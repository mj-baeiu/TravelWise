About TravelWise

How It Started

TravelWise began as a college project inspired by the idea of making travel planning easier, smarter, and more convenient. We wanted to develop a platform that helps travelers prepare for their journeys by providing useful weather information and travel recommendations in one place.

Our Purpose

The purpose of TravelWise is to help travelers make informed decisions before and during their trips. By combining weather forecasts, destination information, and AI-powered travel assistance, the website aims to make trip preparation more convenient and organized.
